Questions.

How much does it cost to open a Momo?

It depends on the size and condition of your space. After a site visit we give you a full setup budget covering fit-out, equipment and opening stock.

Do I need experience running an ice cream shop?

No. We train you and your team on the menu, the counter and day-to-day running before you open.

Who supplies the ice cream?

All Momo ice cream comes from the House of Carmello factory in Sri Lanka, delivered to your parlour.

How big does my space need to be?

Momo works in small shopfronts and mall kiosks as well as larger parlours with seating. Tell us about your space and we'll advise.
